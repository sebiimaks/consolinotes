# Meet consolinotes

Welcome to consolinotes, an independent fork of [FSNotes](https://github.com/glushchenko/fsnotes).

FSNotes was originally developed by Oleksandr Glushchenko and contributors. consolinotes is maintained separately; crediting their work does not imply their involvement in or endorsement of this fork.

## Getting started

Create notes, organize them with folders and tags, and use Markdown to format your writing. The iOS interface retains many features and design elements inherited from FSNotes.
